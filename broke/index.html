    The approach

    Honesty over hype

    This is a working record of improving financial stability, not financial advice and not a promise of a particular outcome.

    • Use actual figures from reviewed records.
    • Separate essential living costs from debt repayments.
    • Show changes over time only when the underlying periods are comparable.
    • Explain setbacks and uncertainty instead of hiding them.

    Privacy by design

    A public summary, not a public ledger

    This site is designed to publish totals and trends only. It must not contain creditor names, account or reference numbers, addresses, identity documents, bank statements, the private workbook, or raw transaction-level records.
