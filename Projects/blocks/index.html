Small pieces. Fresh starts.

Fill a horizontal row to clear it. Every 10 lines brings a faster level. The outline shows where your piece will land. Keep the stack below the top.

Keyboard: focus the board, then ← → move · ↓ soft drop · ↑ / X rotate · Z rotate back · Space drop · P / Escape pause. Use the buttons on a phone or tablet. Leaving the window pauses play.

Single / double / triple / four-line clears earn 100 / 300 / 500 / 800 × your level. Soft drops earn 1 per cell; hard drops earn 2. Your best score is saved in this browser when storage is available.
